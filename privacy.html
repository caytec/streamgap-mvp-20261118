PRIVACY

Simple by design.

StreamGap does not require an account and does not maintain a prompt history or user database. Form inputs are sent to the generation endpoint only when you submit them.

If AI_PROVIDER is configured, submitted form content may be sent to Google Gemini or Groq, depending on the deployment configuration. Their handling of submitted content is governed by their respective privacy policies and service terms. Do not submit personal, confidential, or sensitive information.

Basic temporary rate-limit data may be held in server memory to reduce abuse. The MVP does not intentionally store full prompts or generated results. Generated content stays in your browser unless you choose to copy, share, or download it.

Copy, share, and download actions use browser APIs. The browser, operating system, or selected sharing service may apply its own handling rules after you trigger one of those actions.

StreamGap is an unofficial entertainment tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Twitch.
